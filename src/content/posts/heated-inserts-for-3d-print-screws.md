Worked on inserting magnets and heat inserts for screws today (into 3D prints, for better strength).

I paused the print for the magnet insert and let them have 2 layers printed on top after. The Prusa XL needed help clearing the nozzle upon resume, it did not do that itself.

I sized the hole purely based on the magnet size and that was fine.  The magnets were fairly weak with the 2 layers on top so plan accordingly in the future.

For the brass heat inserts: they worked with the dimensions I measured off the inserts - however, I needed to countersink the insert holes (made a surround that was 1 mm more in DIAMETER, 1 mm deep - should have used 2mm for both those offsets).  However the extra plastic that came out the top was easy to sand away.  [Some sort of drill press setup](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bbsD-3_AP_0&ab_channel=AdafruitIndustries) for the soldering iron would have helped them go in perfectly straight.

I used my older soldering iron ([basic Weller](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00B3SG7F0/?th=1)) with [new tips](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0CP6S47KB) that are sized for different heat insert sizes.

Bought a [very nice Hakko solder station](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0012B8NW8) for my electronics work - had to order the [tips/heaters](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B01HR6ZNH4) separately (which was annoying to find out later) but they are nice and sharp for working on the tiny PCBs.
