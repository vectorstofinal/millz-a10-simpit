LED+ is PWM-dimmed on the high side in the console box, so the whole pour can be ground. The right pot's ends are swapped for routing, so invert A2 in firmware. Check with a meter which toggle lug closes TOP, OFF and OVERRIDE before soldering; the lug positions in the model are approximate. Wire colors match the built panel, except the pushbutton wires, which are not fitted yet. The HARS/SAS toggle has only two terminals and closes at NORM as mounted, so its input is reversed in firmware.

The layers come from the AUX_LTS_split cut files and the board from the v2 milling file. The switches, pots and pushbuttons are modeled from caliper measurements; the knobs are the exact printed design.
