The box is a plain rectangular block with one corner cut at 45° for the seat panel. It looks skewed because the deck under it is tilted in the jet (about 15° nose-up and 13° toward the pilot) while the box is square to the forward bulkhead. In the jet the block and the brake foot are square to the box, which leans the handle 14° outboard and points the shaft 25° into the deck. Here I made the handle square to the plate and laid the shaft down to 8°, so it stays above the plate. Everything is drawn relative to the plate.

Where the gaps are

  1. Absolute scale. Angles are measured. Sizes started as ratios scaled to an assumed 78 mm grip and The Warthog Project's 65 mm wide panel, and are now being corrected against a printed mockup on the console.
  2. Box size. Width, depth and height are estimates. The back and the outboard end don't show in any reference I have, so those are my own shapes.
  3. Shaft angle. The jet's shaft points 25° into the deck. Mine is 8°, so nothing has to go through the plate.
  4. Handle and the AUX LTS panel. In the first version the handle's foot touched the NVIS toggle on the panel behind at full pull. After moving the handle outboard and forward, shortening its foot and raising the plate, the model shows about 8 mm to that toggle. The closest thing is now the left dimmer knob, about 3 mm away at full pull.
  5. Handle shape. Taller and more upright than the jet's. Both changes came from trying the mockup on the console.
  6. Guide block. Square to the shaft and to the plate, so it no longer leans with the box.
  7. Mechanism. None yet. The model is the outside shape only.
  8. Toggle paddle. Shape seen, ribs not modeled.
